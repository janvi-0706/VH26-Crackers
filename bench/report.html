Cost model

actual_worker_seconds = worker_count * duration — the fixed 6-worker pool, paid for regardless of load. naive_scaled_worker_seconds = (offered work-units/sec * duration) / worker_capacity_ups — workers needed, continuously scaled, to stream 100% of that same offered load with zero triage. Both converted to USD at a stated, illustrative $0.36/worker-hour (not tied to any vendor's real pricing — the ratio is the argument, not the absolute figure).
